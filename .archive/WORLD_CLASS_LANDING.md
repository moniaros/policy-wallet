## Overview

This landing page is designed for an **honest MVP launch** with world-class design and copy. Inspired by the existing policywallet.gr website, it maintains professional quality while being truthful about the product's current stage.

## Key Principles

### 1. **Honesty First**
- ✅ No false claims about userbase ("Join thousands" → "Join the future")
- ✅ No fake partnerships or testimonials
- ✅ Clear "Beta" badge indicating MVP status
- ✅ "Early Access" framing instead of "Join 10,000+ users"
- ✅ Focus on vision and benefits, not inflated metrics

### 2. **Professional Quality**
- World-class design inspired by policywallet.gr
- Premium animations and interactions
- Bilingual (Greek primary, English secondary)
- Fully responsive and accessible

### 3. **Trust Building**
- Transparent about being new
- Focus on security and data protection
- Highlight Greek development
- Emphasize user-focused approach
