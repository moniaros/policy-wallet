# Public Greek language and legal-content review — 21 October 2026

## Scope and method

Reviewed the public route tree and its shared copy: home, product and insurance-category pages, pricing, comparison, company/contact, agent and institutional pages, needs questionnaire, trust/methodology/status, 15 guides, glossary and four legal documents. English factual counterparts change with Greek; protected application copy is outside this pass, except the matching statutory-withdrawal wording in the cancellation dialog. Readability for teenagers and older readers does **not** change the adult-only account requirement.

The copy ledger records 432 Greek edits before final verification fixes, with corresponding English corrections where the meaning changed. Short, familiar sentences replace insurance jargon, fear language and absolute promises. Necessary terms are explained at first use. Examples remain examples; document reading is separated from professional advice and a claim decision. Existing visual branding and hero film are retained.

This is a source-backed editorial/legal-risk review, **not a legal opinion or a certification that the business complies with every applicable rule**. A statute describes a duty; it does not prove that a production account, contract or operating process meets it. The unresolved evidence requirements below are deliberately separate from readability improvements.

## Operational/legal evidence still required — not UI preferences

1. **Online withdrawal function:** Law 5317/2026 article 69 added article 3ζα to Law 2251/1994. The official Gazette was downloaded and read (A 108/10 July 2026, pages 5205–5206; commencement article 143). It requires an accessible online declaration, a confirmation action and a receipt including content/date/time on a durable medium. Ordinary subscription cancellation and a mailto link are not evidence of this complete flow. The existing terms' email route remains available; it must not be described as a complete implementation of the new function. Resolve before enabling consumer paid checkout. [Parliament enactment record](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/Nomothetiko-Ergo/Psifisthenta-Nomoschedia?law_id=2fa5316d-9df6-4ff0-8eb9-b479000a6e8f), [official Gazette file](https://ia37rg02wpsa01.blob.core.windows.net/fek/01/2026/20260100108.pdf). This is a genuine functional gap, not an editorial preference.
2. **Provider/account evidence:** archive applicable DPAs, transfer safeguards, retention and training settings for the actual production accounts. Google's Gemini API terms effective 23 March 2026 require Paid Services for EEA clients; provider terms are not proof of the configured account. [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms?hl=en). Do not reintroduce “no training”, “EU-only processing” or certification badges without evidence covering all configured providers and fallbacks.
3. **Regulatory perimeter:** insurance distribution is determined by actual activities, not a footer saying “not an insurer”. Obtain qualified assessment of adviser workflows, personalised outputs, referrals and any assistance with administration/claims before claiming the business is categorically outside IDD. [Bank of Greece — insurance distribution](https://www.bankofgreece.gr/kiries-leitourgies/epopteia/epopteia-idiwtikhs-asfalishs/dianomh-asfalistikwn-proiontwn). This pass removes the categorical self-certification, not the applicable duties.
4. **Retention and special-category data:** policy text is not proof of deletion-job coverage, provider backup retention or a valid legal basis for every person in an uploaded document. Verify those operational records separately. No new consent or compliance certification is inferred from simpler wording.

## UI/UX preferences — do not gate launch

- Use short sentences and concrete questions. Explain «απαλλαγή», «δικαιούχος» and «ασφαλισμένο ποσό» in context.
- Keep the respectful plural. Avoid infantilising older readers or claiming a tested reading-age score.
- Prefer helpful preparation over fear of catastrophe or criticism of advisers.
- Keep the original document and licensed professional as the reference for decisions.
