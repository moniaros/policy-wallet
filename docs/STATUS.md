Counts `active | expiring_soon | action_needed`; excludes `expired | cancelled | unknown_duration | analyzing | deleted`, dedupes re-uploads of the same policy number, and **surfaces** the unknown-duration count in the widget rather than silently dropping it (no-silent-fallback rule).
