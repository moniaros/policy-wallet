## Findings and their fixes

1. **Hero CTA at 2.74:1** — tailwind-merge deleted `text-fg-on-brand` from

## What the components do (verified by hand during build)

- **ReadingDemo**: real roving-tabindex tablist (Arrow/Home/End move AND
  select), `aria-live=polite` completion announcement, replay button, focus
  visible on every control; reduced motion renders the final state instantly;
  the «ΔΕΙΓΜΑ» stamp sits outside the animated region.
- **CoverageTicker**: pauses on hover AND `:focus-within`; reduced motion
  stops the loop and WRAPS the items so nothing is clipped; duplicate list is
  `aria-hidden` with `tabIndex=-1`.
- **PlanRecommender**: native `<input type=range>` (keyboard/touch free),
  `<output>` bound to it, `aria-live` verdict; the chip row is `aria-hidden`
  (decorative duplication of the announced verdict).
- **DeviceFrame**: dot tablist, IntersectionObserver pause, static first
  screen under reduced motion.
- **StatusChip**: glyph + spoken label from the single `STATE_LABELS` source;
  colour never carries meaning alone (§4.2).
- **EmailCapture**: form named via `formAriaLabel` (the destination action),
  16px input floor (iOS focus-zoom).
- Focus style everywhere: 3px `border/focus` outline, 3px offset, shared
  `focusRing` constant.
