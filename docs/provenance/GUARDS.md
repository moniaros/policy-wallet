# PW-PROVENANCE-01 — the guard inventory

One row per guard shipped by this series. A guard enumerates its universe from the filesystem or the
database schema, and ships with a committed probe fixture proven to turn it red. **A guard without a
probe is not a guard.**

| Guard | Universe it enumerates | Probe | What it would have caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ropa-tags-complete` (`tests/unit/ropa-tags-complete.test.ts`) | Every model in `prisma/schema.prisma`, filtered to those holding personal data by the same detector `erasure-covers-personal-data.test.ts` uses — a `@relation` to User under any name, or one of seven plain subject columns. 57 required, 30 explicitly exempt with a written reason. | 5 fixtures: an untagged personal-data model · Art. 9 columns under a non-consent basis · a value outside the vocabulary · a tag missing required keys · `unclear` accepted. Plus a real-tree demonstration: removing `PolicyholderProfile`'s tag fails 2 assertions. | A migration adding a table with a `userId` and no recorded purpose or lawful basis — which is how the Art. 30 gap arose in the first place. Also catches an Art. 9 column list that names a field the model no longer has, and a stale exemption for a model that was deleted. |
| `generated-compliance-docs-current` (`tests/unit/generated-compliance-docs-current.test.ts`) | The two committed generated documents — `docs/compliance/ROPA.md` and `docs/compliance/DPIA-INPUTS.md` — against what `lib/compliance/ropa-report.ts` renders from three sources read at test time: every model and column of `prisma/schema.prisma`, the subprocessor rows `/subprocessors` renders (`lib/legal/legal-content.ts`), and every `lib/services/ai/*-ai.service.ts` (mock excluded) for the options its SDK client is constructed with. Two closed maps throw rather than omit: a published processor with no `PROCESSOR_FEEDS` entry, a provider service with no published row. | 6 cases: the real schema plus a tagged probe model renders a record and a pack that differ from the committed ones · a published processor without a feed entry throws · a live provider service absent from the published list throws · a temp-dir provider file with no published row throws · `baseURL` reads as pinned, `apiKey` alone as SDK default · every live client is constructed with `apiKey` and nothing else (the §14.1 fact, pinned so a change is noticed). **Demonstrated red on the real tree**: run before regeneration, 3 of 10 failed (stale record header, missing pack, probe) — then green. | W4-01 shipped `generate-ropa.ts --check` and nothing in CI ran it: a migration could have added a column and the committed Art. 30 record would have stayed byte-identical with every guard green — the exact drift the record exists to prevent. Also a new AI provider service that nobody added to `/subprocessors`, and an SDK client quietly gaining (or losing) an endpoint pin without the pack saying so. |
