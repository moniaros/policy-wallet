# PW-PROVENANCE-01 — halts

A halt is a decision the series cannot take. Each entry names what stopped, why the loop may not
decide it, and the exact answer that resumes it. Options are presented, never decided. A halt is
closed by **appending** a `### Closed <date>` section — never by deleting the entry.

| Id | Date | What | Why it is a halt | Resumes on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-P1 | 2026-09-07 | **AI providers are not regionally pinned and no zero-retention setting is applied** (§14.1). All three clients are constructed with an API key alone; Gemini uses the global AI Studio endpoint. Complete policy documents — Art. 9 health content included — are processed wherever the provider routes them. | Not a code change. Remediation is either moving Gemini traffic to **Vertex AI with an EU location** (a different SDK, a different auth model, a GCP project) or obtaining **zero-retention terms** on the provider accounts. Both are procurement and infrastructure decisions with cost and contractual consequences. | The owner's decision: (a) Vertex AI + EU location; (b) ZDR terms on the existing accounts; (c) accept and document the residual risk. W0-02/W0-03 shrink the payload either way but do not answer this. |
| H-P2 | 2026-09-07 | **The DPIA itself** (§14.2). Large-scale Art. 9 processing combined with AI evaluation appears to make one mandatory under Art. 35(3)(b). | A legal assessment, not an artifact the loop can generate.
